  About Bridgerton International

Built to Move
the World's Cargo

Bridgerton International Pvt Ltd is a Mohali-based global freight and shipping coordination company operating in the shipping equipment industry. Founded with the mission of delivering Fortune 500-quality logistics management from India, we serve clients across the United States, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific.

What sets us apart is our unique India-USA collaboration model — our Mohali operations team works in close coordination with US citizen partners to ensure seamless freight execution across American markets, container ports, and inland transportation networks.

  Our Journey

How We Grew
Into a Global Freight Force

From a small coordination firm to a globally-recognized freight partner — here's how Bridgerton International built its reputation for excellence.

FOUNDING

Bridgerton International Established

Founded in Sahibzada Ajit Singh Nagar, Mohali, with a focus on freight coordination for US-India trade lanes. The company was built around the vision of premium logistics managed from India.

YEAR 1

US Collaboration Partnership Launched

Formalized our US citizen collaboration model to handle carrier relations, compliance, and last-mile coordination across the American market — enabling full US freight coverage.

YEAR 2

Global Expansion — Middle East & Europe

Expanded freight corridors to include the Gulf region, European ports, and Southeast Asia — growing our active shipping lanes to cover 50+ countries across 6 continents.

PRESENT
