What Is Freight Outsourcing?

Freight outsourcing means delegating specific freight operations functions — dispatch, documentation, tracking, client communication, and data management — to a dedicated external team. Bridgerton International's India-based outsourcing division provides this service to US-based trucking companies, freight brokers, and 3PLs seeking to reduce operational overhead while maintaining service quality.

Our model is built on the proven India-USA business process outsourcing framework — leveraging India's large pool of logistics-trained professionals, time zone advantages, and cost efficiency to deliver operational output at a fraction of US staffing costs.
