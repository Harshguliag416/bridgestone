What Is Port-to-Port Freight?

Port-to-Port freight coordination covers the complete ocean journey of a shipment — from the moment a container is loaded at the origin port to its arrival and clearance at the destination port. While not covering first-mile or last-mile trucking (those are handled by drayage and inland freight), port-to-port ensures complete seamless management of the ocean leg.

Bridgerton International handles ocean carrier booking, bill of lading management, customs documentation, transit monitoring, and arrival coordination — providing our clients with a single point of contact for the ocean freight portion of their supply chain.

Our Port-to-Port Approach

We combine our deep carrier relationships with routing expertise to select the optimal ocean route for your cargo — balancing transit time, cost, and reliability. Whether you're shipping FCL (Full Container Load) or LCL (Less than Container Load), we find the best vessel schedule and negotiate competitive freight rates.

Ocean Carrier Booking

Direct access to major ocean carriers — MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM, Evergreen, and more for best rate selection.

Bill of Lading Management

Complete BOL preparation, verification, and amendment handling — including telex release and express B/L options.

Cargo Tracking

Real-time vessel tracking with milestone updates — vessel departure, in-transit, arrival, and terminal availability.

Customs Documentation

Preparation of all export and import customs documents including commercial invoice, packing list, and COO.

FCL & LCL Options

Full Container Load for large volumes; Less Container Load consolidation for smaller shipments requiring ocean freight.

Inland Connectivity

Seamless handoff to drayage and inland transport at both origin and destination ends through our integrated network.
