What Is Drayage?

Drayage refers to the short-distance transportation of freight — particularly containers — between a seaport, rail yard, or intermodal terminal and a nearby warehouse, distribution center, or consolidation hub. Though the distance is short, drayage is one of the most time-sensitive and cost-impactful elements of the supply chain.

Bridgerton International coordinates drayage services through our US partner network — ensuring your containers are picked up from port or rail immediately after availability, minimizing detention and demurrage charges that can quickly add up.

Drayage in the Supply Chain

Drayage serves as the critical connector between long-haul ocean or rail transport and the final distribution network. Without efficient drayage, containers sit at expensive port terminals accumulating per diem fees and creating downstream supply chain delays. Our proactive drayage management prevents these scenarios.
